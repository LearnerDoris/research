Key Metrics to Watch核心关注点（按重要性排序）

  1. The 2H'26 / HBM4 supply-discipline commentary — matters more than the Q2 print · 2H / HBM4 供给纪律表述 —— 比 Q2 本身更关键
    At ~8x book on a 72% operating margin, the entire debate is durability, not the level of Q2 profit. The market needs to hear that (a) HBM long-term agreements (LTAs) lock 2027 pricing/volume, (b) HBM4 is ramping to plan with SOCAMM2 for Nvidia, and (c) capacity additions (M15X, Yongin) are demand-matched, not a supply race. Any language hinting at conventional-DRAM price stabilization or an ASP peak is the single most dangerous line in the release.
    在 72% 经营利润率上按约 8x book 定价，整个争论是持续性，而非 Q2 利润的绝对水平。市场需要听到：(a) HBM 长期协议（LTA）锁定 2027 年价格/量，(b) HBM4 按计划放量、SOCAMM2 供货 Nvidia，(c) 新增产能（M15X、龙仁）与需求匹配、而非供给竞赛。任何暗示 conventional-DRAM 价格企稳或 ASP 见顶的表述，都是本次财报最危险的一句话。
  2. HBM & server-DRAM ASP trajectory into 2H · HBM 与 server-DRAM 的 ASP 走势
    The Q1→Q2 revenue jump is ASP-led. Watch whether management frames DRAM/HBM pricing as still climbing or "stabilizing." KIS trimmed its Q2 profit estimate specifically on lower-than-expected general-DRAM price increases and LTA-driven ASP stabilization — that is the crack the bears are watching.
    Q1→Q2 的营收跳升由 ASP 主导。关注管理层把 DRAM/HBM 定价描述为"仍在上行"还是"企稳"。KIS 正是因 general-DRAM 涨价不及预期、以及 LTA 带来的 ASP 企稳而下调 Q2 利润预测——这正是空头盯着的裂缝。
  3. NAND / eSSD acceleration — the second leg · NAND / eSSD 加速 —— 第二增长腿
    Consensus has NAND at ~$13.1B (+330% YoY). NAND is more commoditized and less defensible than HBM; a strong NAND quarter broadens the story, but NAND is also the first place a supply response shows up. Watch the eSSD / high-capacity server mix.
    consensus 给 NAND ~$13.1B（+330% YoY）。NAND 相较 HBM 更商品化、护城河更浅；NAND 强劲能拓宽故事，但 NAND 也是供给反应最先显现之处。关注 eSSD / 高容量服务器的 mix。
  4. Capex & capacity guidance (M15X, Yongin, EUV) · 资本开支与产能指引
    Q1 flagged that "this year's investment scale will increase significantly." The bull reading is demand-pull; the bear reading is the start of the next supply glut. The size and discipline of the capex message directly drives the through-cycle earnings the stock should be valued on.
    Q1 已提示"今年投资规模将显著增加"。多头解读是需求拉动；空头解读是下一轮供给过剩的起点。capex 表述的规模与纪律直接决定应据以估值的 through-cycle 盈利。
  5. Shareholder-return & net-cash trajectory · 股东回报与净现金轨迹
    Net cash reached ₩35T in Q1 (from a net-debt position not long ago) and FY2025 returned ₩3,000 DPS + ₩12.2T buyback. A raised return policy would partly offset the peak-cycle valuation concern; silence on it, with capex rising, tilts capital toward the supply race.
    Q1 净现金达 ₩35T（不久前还是净负债），FY2025 派息 ₩3,000 + 回购 ₩12.2T。上调回报政策可部分对冲 peak-cycle 估值担忧；若对此沉默而 capex 上升，则资本更偏向供给竞赛。
