Investment Summary

SK hynix is the world's #1 HBM supplier (56.4% revenue share, IDC Q1'26) and #2 in DRAM (29.1%), the core memory partner to Nvidia through HBM3E, HBM4 and SOCAMM2. The AI-memory shortage has driven an extraordinary print: Q1'26 revenue ₩52.58T (+198% YoY), operating profit ₩37.61T at a 72% margin, on top of record FY2025 revenue ₩97.15T and operating profit ₩47.21T (49% margin) — enough to overtake Samsung on memory profit. The balance sheet flipped to a ₩35T net-cash position; FY2025 returned ₩3,000 DPS plus a ₩12.2T buyback. HBM is booked out for ~three years. On forward earnings the stock is cheap: ~8x our FY2026E, versus a euphoric street at ₩3.41M (Strong Buy, +93%).

The problem is what those earnings are. A 72% operating margin and ~8x book capitalize the peak of the most violently cyclical business in tech — the same franchise posted an operating loss of ₩7.7T in FY2023, just two years ago. Our valuation blends the cheap-on-peak lens (forward P/E ~9–11x, Micron-parity comps → ₩2.04–2.55M) against the through-cycle discipline (normalized P/E ~11–13x on a structurally-higher-but-not-peak HBM floor → ₩0.85–1.50M; 4–5x forward book; a mid-cycle DCF). Weighted, base ₩1.93M; headline target ₩1,950,000 (SKHY ~$160), at the middle of the market-based cluster.

Why NEUTRAL, not BUY: we are not calling a top on a genuinely dominant franchise — we are saying the market already prices a durable super-cycle, leaving balanced risk/reward, while the euphoric street under-writes the normalization risk that memory always eventually delivers. Two hard constraints seal the call: (1) a Q2 print is 7 days out (Jul 29) — our 14-day-catalyst rule bars adding into the event; (2) at ~8x book on peak margins, a single "ASP-stabilizing" line de-rates the stock violently. We would turn Overweight at ~₩1.5M / SKHY ~$123, or on durable through-cycle pricing evidence (firm 2027 LTAs, disciplined capex, HBM4 ramp on plan).

投资摘要

SK hynix 是全球 #1 HBM 供应商（56.4% 收入份额，IDC Q1'26）、DRAM 全球第二（29.1%），通过 HBM3E、HBM4 与 SOCAMM2 成为 Nvidia 的核心内存伙伴。AI-memory 短缺催生了惊人的业绩：Q1'26 营收 ₩52.58T（+198% YoY），operating profit ₩37.61T、margin 72%，叠加创纪录的 FY2025 营收 ₩97.15T、operating profit ₩47.21T（49% margin）——足以在内存利润上反超三星。资产负债表转为 ₩35T 净现金；FY2025 派息 ₩3,000 并回购 ₩12.2T。HBM 已售罄约三年。以 forward earnings 计股价便宜：约 8x 我们的 FY2026E，而卖方一片狂热，目标价 ₩3.41M（Strong Buy，+93%）。

问题在于这些盈利是什么。72% 的 operating margin 与 约 8x book，是在把科技业中周期性最剧烈业务的顶部加以资本化——同一家公司就在两年前的 FY2023 录得 ₩7.7T 经营亏损。我们的估值把"peak 便宜"视角（forward P/E ~9–11x、Micron-parity comps → ₩2.04–2.55M）与 through-cycle 纪律（normalized P/E ~11–13x，采用"结构性抬升但非峰值"的 HBM 盈利底 → ₩0.85–1.50M；4–5x forward book；mid-cycle DCF）加权。加权基准 ₩1.93M；headline 目标价 ₩1,950,000（SKHY ~$160），取 market-based 区间中部。

为何是 NEUTRAL 而非 BUY（大白话）：公司很好，问题只在价格——现在这个价是“按最景气的时候”定的。单看今年利润，股价约 8x forward 并不贵；但这是 72% 利润率、约 8x book 的顶峰利润，而同一家公司 2023 年还亏了 ₩7.7 万亿。综合五种方法，合理价约 ₩1.93M，远低于卖方一致的 ₩3.41M——差距就是卖方没算“利润早晚会回落”（normalization）。两点让我们先等：① 7 月 29 日就出财报（7 天后；我们规矩是大事件前 14 天不加仓）；② 股价高又建在顶峰利润上，很脆弱，财报里一句“价格企稳”就可能大跌。跌到约 ₩1.5M / SKHY ~$123，或看到景气能持续（锁定 2027 HBM 长约、扩产跟着需求走、HBM4 按计划量产），我们就上调至 Overweight。
