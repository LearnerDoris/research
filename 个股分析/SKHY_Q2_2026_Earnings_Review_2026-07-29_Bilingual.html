Why a Record Fell 9%为什么创纪录还跌 9%

Five reasons the market sold a record: (1) it missed an elevated bar — the street wanted ₩84T / ₩64T; "record but not enough." (2) Growth deceleration fears as the base gets huge. (3) The ~10 LTAs cap spot-price upside — locked pricing trades near-term visibility for less spot capture as ASPs climb. (4) The 76% margin is seen as pricing-dependent (DRAM ASP +~30% QoQ, NAND +mid-50%) — fragile if prices cool. (5) A ~₩50T capex plan (M15X pull-forward, Yongin Fab1 early 2027) revived supply-glut worries. Combined, ~₩190T of market value came off. Note the smartphone/PC lines were "temporarily adjusted" as memory was steered to higher-value AI/server demand.
市场卖出创纪录业绩的五个原因：(1) 不及偏高的预期——卖方要 ₩84T / ₩64T，“创纪录但不够”。(2) 随着基数变大的增速放缓担忧。(3) 约 10 份 LTA 压住了现货涨价空间——锁价换来近端可见度，却在 ASP 上行时少赚现货。(4) 76% 的 margin 被视为“靠涨价撑着”（DRAM ASP 环比 +~30%、NAND +mid-50%）——一旦价格降温就脆弱。(5) 约 ₩50T 的 capex 计划（M15X 提前、龙仁 Fab1 于 2027 初）重新点燃供给过剩担忧。合计蒸发约 ₩190T 市值。注意手机/PC 业务被“临时调整”，因内存被优先分配给更高价值的 AI/服务器需求。
