On July 28 Corning reported a clean beat and raised its long-term plan, yet the stock closed −12% on the session (down ~18% intraday — its worst single day since 2002) and dragged the whole optical complex (LITE, COHR, MRVL all −10%+) down with it. Nothing in the results was the problem. The Q3 revenue guide of $4.9–5.0B landed just below the Street's elevated ~$5.0B whisper, and a broad rotation out of AI-infrastructure names did the rest. This is the reaction our July 21 preview called for — and it has finally handed us the entry the initiation was waiting for. 7 月 28 日康宁交出干净的 beat 并上调了长期计划，股价当日却收跌约 12%（盘中一度跌约 18%，为 2002 年以来最差单日），并带动整个 optical 板块（LITE、COHR、MRVL 均跌逾 10%）。问题不在业绩本身：Q3 营收指引 $4.9–5.0B 略低于市场约 $5.0B 的偏高预期，加上 AI-infrastructure 板块的整体轮动，共同促成了这次抛售。这正是我们 7 月 21 日前瞻所预判的反应——也终于给出了首次覆盖时一直在等的入场点。

02 Why a beat sold off ~18% 为何 beat 却大跌

The guidance "whiff" against an elevated whisper the trigger
Q3 sales guided $4.9–5.0B (+16% YoY). Above the ~$4.8–4.9B we flagged as necessary — but the buy-side had crept to ~$5.0B, so the midpoint read as a shortfall. When consensus already equals guidance, "in-line" is a disappointment. Exactly the mechanism our preview warned about. Q3 营收指引 $4.9–5.0B（+16%），高于我们此前标注的约 $4.8–4.9B 门槛，却低于买方悄然升至的约 $5.0B 预期，中值因此被读作不及。当 consensus 已等于指引，「符合预期」即是失望——正是前瞻警示的机制。
A broad AI-infrastructure rotation amplifier
GLW did not fall alone — Lumentum, Coherent, Marvell and the optical complex all dropped 10%+ the same day. After a ~391% run, a high-beta (~1.1) momentum name in a de-rating sector was the first thing sold. Analysts explicitly attribute the move to rotation and expectations, not to any change in fundamentals. A "memory-price" cost headwind was cited as a minor added worry. GLW 并非独跌——Lumentum、Coherent、Marvell 及整个 optical 板块当日同跌逾 10%。在约 391% 涨幅之后，一只高 beta（约 1.1）动量股在板块 de-rating 中最先被卖出。分析师明确将下跌归因于轮动与预期，而非基本面变化；「memory 价格」成本逆风被视为次要额外担忧。
Priced for perfection — no margin of safety to absorb it the setup
This is the initiation thesis in one event: at 36× forward, the stock had zero cushion. Great-but-not-flawless was enough to compress the multiple hard. The problem was never the business; it was the price the business was carrying. 这正是首次覆盖论点在一次事件中的体现：36× forward 之下毫无缓冲，「优秀但非完美」就足以令估值大幅收缩。问题从来不是生意，而是生意所背负的价格。
✓ The July 21 preview called this — almost to the dollar
We rated it "do not add," said the Q3 guide would matter more than the print, put the bear-case reaction at $126–138, and pre-committed to turning Overweight at $120–130 or on Q2 re-acceleration evidence. The stock closed at $126.01 — the bottom of that band — and delivered both upgrade triggers at once. We are not chasing a dip; we are executing a plan set in writing before the event. 我们此前评级「不建议加仓」，指出 Q3 指引比当季数字更重要，将 bear 情景反应定为 $126–138，并预先承诺在 $120–130 或出现 Q2 再加速证据时上调至 Overweight。股价收于 $126.01——正是该区间下沿——且两个上调触发条件同时满足。这不是追跌，而是执行事前书面设定的计划。

05 What changed on the bear side — the new risks 空头侧的变化——新增风险

Capital intensity — the "capital-efficient Springboard" story is now qualified
The NVIDIA agreement means a 10× optical-manufacturing expansion and 50%+ more fiber capacity. That is a real capex step-up, and it partly undercuts the original low-capital-intensity appeal — and raises overbuild risk if AI capex ever digests. Watch the next capex guide closely. NVIDIA 协议意味着光学产能扩 10 倍、光纤产能增逾 50%，这是实实在在的 capex 台阶，部分削弱了原本「低资本消耗」的吸引力，并在 AI capex 一旦消化时抬高过度建设风险。需密切关注下一次 capex 指引。
Momentum & rotation — a 2002-scale single-day break leaves technical damage
This is an upgrade to accumulate into the rotation, not a call for a one-day bounce. The AI-infrastructure de-rating can persist, and a name this heavily sold can stay heavy for weeks. Size in, don't reach. 这是在轮动之中逐步买入的上调，而非押注次日反弹。AI-infrastructure 的 de-rating 可能持续，遭如此重卖的个股可能疲弱数周。分批建仓，勿追。
Bottom line · 结论
Corning did the hard part — it grew, it raised the plan, it won NVIDIA and Amazon — and the market handed us a 25% cheaper entry for the trouble. That is the asymmetry we wanted and did not have at $153. Upgrade to Overweight, target $158; accumulate into the AI-optical rotation. 康宁做成了最难的部分——增长、上调计划、赢得 NVIDIA 与 Amazon——市场却为此给了我们便宜 25% 的入场点。这正是我们在 $153 时没有、如今得到的非对称性。上调至 Overweight，目标价 $158；在 AI-optical 轮动中逐步买入。
