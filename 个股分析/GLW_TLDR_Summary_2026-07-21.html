Investment Summary

Corning delivered FY2025A revenue of $15.63B (+19.1% YoY) and Q1 FY2026 core sales of $4.345B (+18% YoY) with core EPS of $0.70 (+30%). The engine is Optical Communications, +36% YoY, now the largest segment, carried by AI-datacenter demand: an up-to-$6B multi-year Meta fiber agreement plus two additional hyperscale long-term deals. Core operating margin hit 20.2% and core gross margin 39.1%. The Springboard plan targets +$6.5B incremental annualized sales to a ~$20B run-rate exiting 2026, largely off existing capacity. Fundamentals are genuinely strong and still improving.

The problem is valuation math, not business quality. Our DCF (5-yr unlevered FCF, WACC 9.0%, g 3.0%) yields only $59/share — the same structural limitation we flagged on ASML and TSM: a five-year window handing off to a 3% perpetuity cannot carry a datacenter-optical super-cycle or a Photonics MAP that barely exists in the base. We therefore weight it just 10%. Our 6-peer comps (APH, COHR, LITE, FN, CIEN, TEL) imply $134–$163 at 28–34x 2028E core EPS of $4.79 — yet GLW already trades at 36.0x forward vs a 30.7x peer median. Weighted across four methods: $138 base, $145 target (set at the middle of the market-based cluster of $143–148).

Why HOLD, not SELL: the ~$70/share gap to Street consensus ($214 avg, 15 analysts, Buy) is explainable, not irrational — it hinges on (1) whether Springboard's $20B exit-run-rate and the $10B-by-2030 Photonics MAP scale as guided, and (2) whether Corning's multiple premium to peers is durable as the optical mix keeps rising. We don't think our numbers are "wrong" — we think the market may be underwriting a more optimistic resolution than we're willing to assume. At $153.10 the market is pricing almost exactly our 30/50/20 bull/base/bear outcome: a fair price, not a cheap one. We would turn Overweight at $120–130.

Key Catalysts (6–12mo)

  • Q2 FY2026 earnings — Jul 28, 2026 (1 week out): consensus core EPS $0.754 vs guidance $0.73–0.77 — no cushion. The Q3 guide matters more than the print: it must reach ~$4.8–4.9B to keep the $20B exit run-rate intact

  • Solar-wafer margin ramp post-maintenance (~$30M Q2 expense); carrier/telco capex trajectory

Key Risks

  • Upside to $145: Optical sustains 35%+ growth; a fourth hyperscale deal lands; Springboard beats toward a >$22B run-rate; Photonics MAP scales faster than modeled; the market keeps paying a growth multiple for an increasingly optical-weighted Corning
  • Downside to $145: hyperscaler capex digestion (Optical is now the largest segment and most concentrated — Meta plus two customers); carrier/telco deferral persists as in 2023–24; display pricing erosion in Glass Innovations (+1% in Q1); utilization stalls and strands fixed-cost capacity, breaking the margin-expansion thesis; multiple compresses toward the industrial peer group; CEO succession (Weeks, CEO since 2005); Apple single-customer concentration in Specialty Materials

投资摘要

康宁 FY2025A 营收 $15.63B（+19.1% YoY），Q1 FY2026 core sales $4.345B（+18% YoY），core EPS $0.70（+30%）。增长引擎是 Optical Communications，同比 +36%，现已成为最大 segment，由 AI 数据中心需求驱动：与 Meta 签订最高达 $6B 的多年期光纤协议，另有两家 hyperscaler 长期合同。core operating margin 达 20.2%，core gross margin 39.1%。Springboard 计划目标新增 $6.5B 年化销售、2026 年底达约 $20B run-rate，且主要依托既有产能实现。基本面确实强劲且仍在改善。

问题不在于公司质地，而在于估值测算。我们的 DCF（五年 unlevered FCF，WACC 9.0%，g 3.0%）仅得出 每股 $59——这与我们在 ASML、TSM 报告中标注的结构性局限相同：五年显性期加 3% 永续增长的框架，无法承载 datacenter-optical 超级周期，也无法承载在基期几乎尚不存在的 Photonics MAP，故仅给予 10% 权重。基于 6 家同业（APH、COHR、LITE、FN、CIEN、TEL）的 comps，以 28–34x 乘 2028E core EPS $4.79，隐含 每股 $134–$163——而 GLW 当前已交易于 36.0x forward，高于 30.7x 的同业中值。四方法加权：基准 $138，目标价 $145（取 market-based 区间 $143–148 的中部）。

为何是 HOLD 而非 SELL：与卖方一致预期（均值 $214，15 位分析师，Buy）约每股 $70 的差距是可以解释的，而非不合理——核心取决于（1）Springboard 的 $20B 年化 run-rate 与 2030 年 $10B 的 Photonics MAP 能否如指引般放量，（2）随着 optical 占比持续上升，康宁相对同业的估值溢价是否具备持久性。我们不认为自己的测算"错了"——我们认为市场可能对上述问题给出了比我们愿意假设的更乐观的答案。在 $153.10，市场定价几乎恰好等于我们 30/50/20 的 bull/base/bear 结果：这是公允价，而非便宜价。若回调至 $120–130，我们将上调至 Overweight。
