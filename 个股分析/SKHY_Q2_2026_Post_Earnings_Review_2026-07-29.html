On July 29 SK hynix reported an all-time record — revenue ₩79.3T (+257% YoY, +51% QoQ) and operating profit ₩60.5T at a 76% margin — yet the ADR fell ~9% to $130.17 (the Seoul line briefly −11%), erasing ~₩190T of market value. Nothing in the results was weak. The print simply landed ~5% below an elevated Street (≈₩84T sales / ₩64T OP), a ~₩50T capex plan revived supply-glut fears, and a 76% margin built on DRAM ASP +~30% / NAND +mid-50% QoQ looked pricing-dependent. This is the reaction our July 22 preview called for — and it hands us a meaningfully better entry, without yet resolving the structural-vs-cyclical question. 7 月 29 日 SK hynix 交出历史新高——营收 ₩79.3T（+257% YoY、+51% QoQ）、operating profit ₩60.5T、margin 76%——ADR 却跌约 9% 至 $130.17（首尔线盘中一度 −11%），蒸发约 ₩190T 市值。业绩本身并不弱：只是较偏高的卖方预期低约 5%（≈₩84T 营收 / ₩64T OP），约 ₩50T 的 capex 计划重新点燃供给过剩担忧，且 76% 的 margin 建立在 DRAM ASP 环比 +~30% / NAND +mid-50% 之上、显得靠涨价撑着。这正是我们 7 月 22 日前瞻预判的反应——给出明显更好的入场点，但结构性 vs 周期性的问题仍未解决。

02 Why a record sold off ~9% 为何创纪录还大跌

A ~5% miss against an elevated whisper the trigger
Revenue ₩79.3T and OP ₩60.5T were records — but the buy-side had crept to ≈₩84T / ₩64T. When expectations run ahead of even a parabolic print, "record but not enough" sells off. OP landed at the low end of the ₩60–70T range — almost exactly the KIS bear estimate (₩60.4T) we cited in the preview. 营收 ₩79.3T、OP ₩60.5T 均创纪录——但买方已悄然升至 ≈₩84T / ₩64T。当预期跑在抛物线业绩之前，“创纪录但不够”就会被卖出。OP 落在 ₩60–70T 区间低端，几乎正好等于我们前瞻引用的 KIS 空头值（₩60.4T）。
A ~₩50T capex plan — the supply-race signal amplifier
Management guided 2026 capex to the high-₩40T range, pulling forward M15X and accelerating Yongin Fab1 into early 2027. In a memory cycle, a capex step-up at the peak is exactly what the bears watch for — the seed of the next glut. This is one of the downgrade triggers we named in the initiation, and it directly qualifies the bull case. 管理层将 2026 capex 指引至 ₩40T 高段，提前 M15X、把龙仁 Fab1 加速至 2027 年初。在内存周期里，顶峰时的 capex 抬升正是空头盯着的信号——下一轮过剩的种子。这是我们首次覆盖中列出的下调触发之一，直接给多头逻辑打了折扣。
Priced on peak margins — no cushion to absorb it the setup
This is the initiation thesis in one event: at ~8× book on a 72–76% margin, the stock had no margin of safety. A record-but-imperfect quarter, with margin visibly ASP-dependent (DRAM +30% / NAND +mid-50% QoQ) and LTAs now capping spot upside, was enough to compress hard. The problem was never the business; it was the price on peak-cycle earnings. 这正是首次覆盖论点在一次事件中的体现：在 72–76% margin 上按约 8× book 定价，毫无安全边际。一个“创纪录但不完美”的季度，margin 明显靠 ASP（DRAM +30% / NAND +mid-50%）、且 LTA 开始封顶现货上行，就足以令估值大幅收缩。问题从不是生意，而是给顶峰盈利定的价。
✓ The July 22 preview called this — and resolved our data flag
We rated it "do not chase," said the 2H/HBM4 commentary would matter more than the print, put the bear/base reaction at −6% to −22%, and pre-committed to Overweight at ₩1.5M / SKHY ~$123, or on durable through-cycle evidence. The ADR closed −9% at $130.17 — inside that band. Bottom line · 结论
SK hynix did the hard part — a record quarter, HBM4 into mass production, ~10 LTAs locked — and the sell-off handed us a 23% cheaper entry to our target. But a ₩50T capex plan at the cycle peak, and a 76% margin that leans on continued price gains, keep the normalization debate open. Reiterate NEUTRAL, target ₩1,950,000 / $160. Upgrade to Overweight at ₩1.5M / SKHY ~$123, or on Q3 evidence the LTAs defend pricing. SK hynix 做成了最难的部分——创纪录、HBM4 量产、锁定约 10 份 LTA——抛售也给了我们相对目标便宜 23% 的入场点。但顶峰时的 ₩50T capex，以及靠持续涨价撑着的 76% margin，让 normalization 之争仍未定。维持 NEUTRAL，目标价 ₩1,950,000 / $160。若回落至 ₩1.5M / SKHY ~$123，或 Q3 证明 LTA 守住价格，则上调至 Overweight。
