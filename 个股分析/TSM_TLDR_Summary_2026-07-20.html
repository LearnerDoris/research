Investment Summary

Every path through the AI buildout runs through TSMC. Foundry share reached 72.3% in 1Q26 (11x the #2 player), an estimated >90% at ≤5nm, and effectively ~100% of AI accelerator fabrication and CoWoS advanced packaging — NVIDIA, AMD, and every hyperscaler custom ASIC alike. Owning TSM is a bet on total AI compute demand, not on any single chip vendor winning. The customer ledger flipped in 2025: NVIDIA (~19%) overtook Apple (~17.5%) as the largest customer for the first time in over a decade, and HPC now stands at 58% of revenue heading toward ~73% by 2030E in our base case.

The constraint is supply, not demand. 2nm was effectively sold out for 2026 before it shipped; N2/A16 capacity is planned at ~70% CAGR through 2028; CoWoS is sold out through 2026 with lead times into 2027. Q2 confirmed the economics: GM 67.7%, OM 60.3%, both all-time highs, with N2 already contributing 3% of wafer revenue in its first meaningful quarter and advanced nodes (≤7nm) at 77% of the mix.

Why the discount persists — and why we think it overcompensates: at ~18.8x NTM P/E against a 29.2x peer median, TSM is the second-cheapest name in the AI complex despite the highest share, ~36% growth, and 67% gross margins. The gap prices Taiwan-strait tail risk, two-customer concentration (~36% of revenue), and tariff friction. Those risks are real and chronic — but they are not new, and Q2's guidance raise widened the fundamental gap while the multiple compressed. Our DCF yields only $287/ADR, a known structural limitation (a 5-year window plus Gordon Growth cannot carry mid-40s% AI revenue CAGR guidance or post-window capex normalization), so it carries just 10% weight; the target leans on trading comps.

Key Catalysts (6–12mo)

  • Q3 FY2026 earnings — mid-Oct 2026: tests the guided GM step-down to ~65% and whether N2 dilution stays within the official 3–4pp
  • FY2027 capex guidance — Jan 2027 call: the single cleanest read on whether AI demand is still supply-constrained
  • Hyperscaler FY2027 capex guidance (Oct 2026 – Feb 2027) — largest exogenous swing factor for HPC, 58% of revenue
  • N2P / A16 ramp and 2027 wafer pricing — each ~10% leading-edge hike ≈ +2pp gross margin
  • Arizona milestones & Section 232 exemption detail — every tariff escalation strengthens the US-fab franchise

Key Risks

  • Taiwan-strait concentration (extreme severity, low-moderate probability): all sub-3nm capacity on one island; unhedgeable. Arizona/Kumamoto/Dresden diversify but stay ~2 generations behind by policy
  • AI capex digestion (the central earnings risk): HPC = 58% of revenue; a 2027 pause would cut growth to single digits with 3–6pp margin compression (bear case $284)
  • Margin normalization: management now quantifies 2nm ramp dilution at 3–4pp and overseas fabs at a further 2–4pp over coming years; Q2's 67.7% is likely a local peak
  • NTD appreciation: revenue ~100% USD vs. ~75% NTD costs — every 1% of appreciation ≈ −0.4pp gross margin, and largely unhedgeable at TSMC's scale
  • Customer concentration & policy steering: top-two ≈ 36% of revenue; Washington holds Intel equity plus tariff leverage and could direct marginal US volumes to 18A

投资摘要

AI 基建的每一条路径都必须经过台积电。1Q26 代工份额达 72.3%（为第二名的 11 倍），5nm 及以下估计 >90%，AI 加速器晶圆制造与 CoWoS 先进封装实质上接近 100%——NVIDIA、AMD 与所有云厂商自研 ASIC 无一例外。持有 TSM 等于做多「AI 算力总需求」本身，而无需押注任何单一芯片厂商的胜负。2025 年客户榜单翻页：NVIDIA（约 19%）十余年来首次超越 Apple（约 17.5%）成为第一大客户；HPC 现占营收 58%，Base 情景下 2030E 升至约 73%。

受限的是供给，不是需求。2nm 在出货前就已实质售罄；N2/A16 产能规划 2026–2028 以约 70% CAGR 扩张；CoWoS 已售罄至 2026 年底、交期排入 2027 年。Q2 财报确认了这套经济学：Gross Margin 67.7%、Operating Margin 60.3% 双双创历史新高，N2 在首个有意义的季度即贡献 3% 晶圆收入，先进制程（≤7nm）占比升至 77%。

折价为何存在，以及我们为何认为它已过度补偿：以约 18.8x NTM P/E 对 同业中值 29.2x，TSM 是 AI 复合体中第二便宜的标的——尽管拥有最高份额、约 36% 的增速与 67% 的毛利率。这一价差定价了台海尾部风险、双客户集中（约占营收 36%）与关税摩擦。这些风险真实且长期存在，但并非新增变量；而 Q2 的指引上调在倍数收缩的同时反而拉大了基本面差距。我们的 DCF 仅得出 $287/ADR，这是一项已知的结构性局限（五年显性期 + Gordon Growth 无法承载管理层 AI 收入 mid-40s% CAGR 指引与显性期后 capex 强度正常化），因此仅占 10% 权重，目标价主要依赖 trading comps。

关键催化剂（未来6-12个月）

  • Q3 FY2026 财报 — 2026年10月中旬：检验指引中毛利率回落至约 65% 的成色，以及 N2 稀释是否守在官方 3–4pp 口径内
  • FY2027 资本开支指引 — 2027年1月法说会：判断 AI 需求是否仍处供给受限状态的最直接读数
  • 云厂商 FY2027 资本开支指引（2026年10月–2027年2月）——HPC 平台（58% 营收）最大的外生变量
  • N2P / A16 放量与 2027 年晶圆定价——先进制程每提价约 10% ≈ Gross Margin +2pp
  • 亚利桑那进度与 Section 232 关税豁免细则——每一次关税升级反而强化台积电美国厂的特许价值

关键风险

  • 台海地缘集中（严重度极高、概率低-中）：所有 sub-3nm 产能集中于一岛，不可对冲。亚利桑那/熊本/德累斯顿虽在分散，但按政策落后约两代
  • AI capex 消化（核心盈利风险）：HPC 占营收 58%；若 2027 年出现暂停，增速或降至个位数并伴随 3–6pp 利润率压缩（Bear 情景 $284）
  • Margin 正常化：管理层已量化 2nm 爬坡稀释 3–4pp、海外厂未来数年再压 2–4pp；Q2 的 67.7% 大概率是本轮局部峰值
  • 新台币升值：收入约 100% 以美元计价、成本约 75% 为新台币——每升值 1% ≈ Gross Margin −0.4pp，且以台积电体量基本无法有效对冲
  • 客户集中与政策引导：前两大客户约占营收 36%；华盛顿持有 Intel 股权并握有关税杠杆，可能将美国边际订单导向 18A
