Investment Summary

Seagate is one of two survivors of the HDD duopoly (with Western Digital) and the clear HAMR/Mozaic technology leader — the mass-storage backbone of the AI-datacenter build-out. The AI-nearline shortage has driven a historic run: Q3 FY2026 revenue $3.11B (+44% YoY), a record 47% gross margin, ~37.5% operating margin, ~$953M FCF (a decade high), non-GAAP EPS $4.10 (vs $3.48 consensus). FY2026 is tracking to ~$12.0B revenue / ~$14.8 non-GAAP EPS, eclipsing the FY2022 peak. Uniquely, nearline is contractually locked through fiscal 2027 (build-to-order contracts set config and price); management raised the annual growth target to ≥20%.

The problem is price. At $871 the stock trades at ~59x FY2026E / ~40x FY2027E non-GAAP earnings — a premium to WDC (~27x) and to its own history — capitalizing record peak-cycle margins one cycle after a FY2023 GAAP loss. Our bridge blends the structural-winner lens (forward P/E on contracted earnings, comps → $720–$1,060) against through-cycle discipline (normalized P/E ~18–26x on a structurally-higher-but-not-peak floor → $280–$520; a mid-cycle DCF ~$220). Weighted base ~$747; headline target $800, the upper end of the market-based cluster.

Why NEUTRAL, not BUY: we are not calling a top on a genuinely superior franchise — we are saying the market already prices AI-storage as permanent, leaving no margin of safety and a negative asymmetry. Two hard constraints seal the call: (1) a fiscal Q4 + full-year print is ~5 days out (Jul 28) — our 14-day-catalyst rule bars adding into the event; (2) at ~40x forward on peak margins, one line on nearline-pricing "normalizing" de-rates the stock violently. We would turn Overweight at ~$600 (forward P/E ~30x, near WDC), or on hard evidence the demand is structurally non-cyclical (FY2028+ BTO extensions).

投资摘要

Seagate（希捷）是 HDD 双寡头中的两家幸存者之一（另一家为 Western Digital），也是明确的 HAMR/Mozaic 技术领导者——AI 数据中心建设的大容量存储基座。AI-nearline 短缺催生了历史性业绩：Q3 FY2026 营收 $3.11B（+44% YoY）、record 47% gross margin、~37.5% operating margin、~$953M FCF（十年新高）、non-GAAP EPS $4.10（vs 一致预期 $3.48）。FY2026 全年 tracking 至 ~$12.0B 营收 / ~$14.8 non-GAAP EPS，超过 FY2022 峰值。罕见的是，nearline 已通过 build-to-order 合同锁定至 fiscal 2027（同时锁定配置与价格）；管理层把年度增长目标上调至 ≥20%。

问题在于价格。$871 对应 ~59x FY2026E / ~40x FY2027E non-GAAP earnings——较 WDC（~27x）与自身历史都是溢价，是在把 record peak-cycle margins 资本化，而上一个周期（FY2023）它还录得 GAAP 亏损。我们的估值桥把"结构性赢家"视角（对已签约盈利的 forward P/E、comps → $720–$1,060）与 through-cycle 纪律（normalized P/E ~18–26x，采用"结构性抬升但非峰值"的盈利底 → $280–$520；mid-cycle DCF ~$220）加权。加权基准 ~$747；headline 目标价 $800，取 market-based 区间上沿。

为何是 NEUTRAL 而非 BUY：我们并非对一个真正更优的特许经营权"喊顶"——而是认为市场已经把 AI-storage 当作永久性来定价，因此没有 margin of safety、且不对称偏负。两条硬约束锁定评级：(1) fiscal Q4 + 全年财报约 5 天后公布（7 月 28 日）——14 天催化剂规则禁止在事件前加仓；(2) 在 peak margin 的 ~40x forward 上，一句 nearline 定价"企稳"就会让股价剧烈 de-rate。若回落至 ~$600（forward P/E ~30x，接近 WDC），或出现需求结构性非周期化的证据（FY2028+ 的 BTO 续约），我们将上调至 Overweight。
